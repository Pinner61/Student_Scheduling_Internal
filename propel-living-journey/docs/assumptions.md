# Assumptions for Des’s approval

These choices are in the review prototype so the design team has something concrete to react to. They are not new published facts. The longer walkthrough is `DES_REVIEW.md`. The contact gap is `docs/contact-form.md`.

Sources used on 2 November 2026:

- https://universitycollege.asu.edu/propel
- https://universitycollege.asu.edu/projects/project-propel
- https://brandguide.asu.edu/
- https://asu.github.io/asu-unity-stack/

## Content

1. **Tense follows the page it came from.** Year one uses the marketing page’s past-tense summary, including the published sentence about measurable improvement. That page gives no figure, and none was added. Year two stays in the page’s present tense. Year three stays in its future tense. The project listing’s year-one items are labeled “Year-one goals” and are not described as results.

2. **The timeline is provisional.** November 2025 through November 2028 is read from the listing’s start `11/25` and end `11/28`. No individual milestone dates were invented.

3. **Tier numbering.** The page names Universal, Targeted, and Intensive support, and it calls the 500-student pilot “tier three.” This prototype uses Tier 3 for universal support, Tier 2 for targeted support, and Tier 1 for intensive support. The tier section says that directly.

4. **The named system is Analytics Integrator 2.0.** Signals 2.0 is not used.

5. **No invented success metrics.** There are no percentages, retention deltas, revenue figures, or testimonials. The 500 LEAD students are the published pilot cohort.

6. **Pillar and pathway graphics are explanatory pictures.** Each pillar line runs to the student. The year line runs from infrastructure to full implementation. Neither graphic is a technical architecture.

7. **People and photographs.** Names and titles match the PROPEL page. Portraits and pillar illustrations are the files from that page, saved in `public/media/`. Phil Arcuria’s published portrait is low resolution. No biographies were written.

8. **The donor is unnamed.** The page thanks “a donor” and does not give a name. None was added.

9. **Contact delivery is not configured.** The form validates and then says the message was not sent. It does not post to the live webform.

10. **Chrome.** The local header uses the public Webspark horizontal logo and is hidden with `data-show-chrome="false"` when Drupal already supplies the global header and footer.
