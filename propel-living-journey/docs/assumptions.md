# Assumptions for Des’s approval

These choices are in the prototype so the design team has something concrete to react to. They are not published facts beyond the sources named here.

Sources used:

- https://universitycollege.asu.edu/propel
- https://universitycollege.asu.edu/projects/project-propel
- https://brandguide.asu.edu/ (color pairings and the ASU grayscale)
- https://asu.github.io/asu-unity-stack/ (Unity Design System as the accessibility and component reference)
- https://president.asu.edu/ (editorial tone and scale, not a layout to copy)

## Content

1. **Voice is prospective.** The marketing page sometimes uses past tense (“established”, “demonstrated measurable improvement”). This prototype describes aims and published goals. It does not state those lines as results.

2. **The timeline is provisional.** November 2025 through November 2028 comes from the public project listing. No individual milestone dates were invented. The public page says once: “Dates within this window are subject to confirmation.” Longer editorial notes stay in this file.

3. **Tier numbering is unresolved.** The project listing describes Tier 3 as self-directed videos. The marketing page describes universal, targeted, and intensive support. The year map follows the project brief: a Tier 3 pilot in year one, Tiers 1 and 2 in year two, and all three tiers refined in year three. Both descriptions appear in the expandable notes. The team should confirm the mapping before this is public.

4. **The named system is Analytics Integrator 2.0.** The project listing also mentions Signals 2.0. The interface uses Analytics Integrator 2.0 only, matching the brief and the marketing page. Signals 2.0 is omitted until the team confirms the relationship.

5. **No success metrics.** Completion percentages, retention, revenue, and student-success statistics are intentionally absent, including where the marketing page refers to measurable improvement without a number.

6. **Year-one details are published goals.** Approximately 30 microlearning videos, Canvas, LEAD and Explore, the Flourishing Measure (VanderWeele, 2017), and a cohort of about 500 LEAD students appear only as goals from the project listing.

7. **Year-two extras stay in the notes.** Team teaching and AI-driven chatbots are mentioned on the public PROPEL page. They appear in “Year two in more detail,” not in the initiative list. The diagram labels “Tiers,” “Communities,” and “Coaches” are shorthand for those initiatives, not new program names.

8. **Pillar copy is shortened from the official site.** The Life by Design question “What matters most to you?” is kept. The diagram is one approach with four parts. It does not claim a dependency order.

9. **The closing does not add a call to action.** It links to the official PROPEL website. Donor, team, and “let’s connect” language from the marketing page is omitted.

10. **The moving line is a reading position.** It advances with the story. The page no longer explains that in a disclaimer. It is not a completion meter.

## Design

11. **The prototype header is not the university header.** “PROPEL / The Living Journey” is a text identifier. No ASU logo and no invented PROPEL logo are drawn. An official page should use the Webspark header and `data-show-chrome="false"`.

12. **Type is Arial.** That matches the current ASU web standard. Licensed faces such as Neue Haas Grotesk are not loaded.

13. **Gold is a graphic color.** It marks the path, nodes, rules, and the active stage. It is not used as text on white.

14. **The closing band is black with white text and a gold rule.** Maroon text is not placed on black.

15. **Corners stay square.** Unity components often use a small radius. This prototype uses sharp edges so the editorial layout stays distinct. That can change if the page must match UDS buttons exactly.

16. **The experience is a separate prototype.** It does not replace the scheduling application in this repository, and it does not change a live Drupal theme.
